# 6.4 Block Project Self Review 💚

## Purpose:

Week 6 introduces no new lab work on purpose: you have earned the perspective to judge your own system. In this assignment you will finish and refine your block project, then critique it honestly against the same rubric your instructor will use in Week 7. An engineer who can accurately assess their own work before the reviewer does is an engineer who gets trusted with bigger systems.

Because your project lives in GitHub, your review is grounded in evidence — commits, Actions runs, manifests, and Terraform — not in what you remember doing.

## Instructions:

1. Finish and refine your project, then critique your own work.

2. Using the rubric for the **Final Block Project from Week Seven**, grade your project as it stands today.
   1. Be specific and use all the columns and all the ratings.
   2. Give yourself a grade using the rubric.
   3. For each rubric column and category, point to **evidence in your repository**: a manifest, a Terraform file, a green (or red) GitHub Actions run, a working `kubectl` demonstration, or an architecture diagram. A claim without a link is just a hope.

3. In a review document (`SELF-REVIEW.md` in your repository, or a Word document), justify why you gave yourself the grade in each column and category and why you believe you qualify for that grade with your current Week Six project (before the new adjustments you will make). Include:
   1. A paragraph about **what you did well**.
   2. A second paragraph about **what you need to do to improve your project**. Be specific about each improvement you will make — including any security gaps (plain-text credentials? public load balancers still running? indefinitely retained logs?) revealed by this week's stewardship discussion.
   3. A third paragraph about **what you learned during this process**, including what you think is better human-done and what is better AI-done. This is important to help you understand humanizing your work and knowledge.

4. **Finish this assignment before class.** This is necessary to prepare for the in-class peer review (6.5).

5. After you've given yourself a grade and completed this assignment, revise your Block Project in preparation for the peer review.

6. **Submit** your review document to Canvas. It must contain:
   1. The grade you think you qualify for right now (before you make revisions).
   2. Specific information about what grade you qualify for in each column and category, with repository evidence.
   3. Paragraph on what you did well.
   4. Paragraph on what you need to do to improve your project before next week.
   5. Paragraph on human-done vs. AI-done.

## Grading

You may earn up to full points on this assignment by completing all requirements listed in the rubric to the necessary quality.
